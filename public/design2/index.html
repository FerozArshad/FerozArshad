/ 06 — The Q&A

Press conference.

The questions that get asked at every screening — answered up front so we can spend our time on the brief.

What kind of projects do you take on?
Full-stack SaaS builds, headless or Shopify e-commerce that needs to convert, n8n & AI automation systems, and strategic design work for founder-led teams. Engagements range from 4-week scoped sprints to multi-month builds where I act as your senior technical partner.

Do you work alone or with a team?
Most engagements are solo — design, engineering and strategy in one head means fewer handoffs and faster ship cycles. For larger builds I assemble a small, hand-picked crew I've shipped with before.

How do you compare to an agency?
Agencies sell time and handoffs. I sell outcomes. You talk to the person writing the code, drawing the screens, and questioning the brief — not three account managers and a junior. Same senior-level work, half the surface area.

What does an engagement cost?
Scoped sprints start around $8k. Multi-month builds and retainer arrangements are priced by outcome. I share a clear scope, deliverable list and weekly rhythm before any contract is signed.

Where are you based and what timezones do you work?
Based in Karachi (UTC+5). I keep four hours overlap with EU mornings and US east-coast afternoons, and I respond inside one business day.

How long until I see something real?
You see a strategy memo and clickable prototype in week one. Working software in production within four to six weeks for most scoped sprints.

Do you sign NDAs and IP transfers?
Yes. Standard mutual NDA up front, full IP assignment on delivery. The code, the design files and the documentation are yours.
