/ 06 · Objections handled

The questions
that always come up.

Honest answers, written by me. If your question isn't here, just ask in the brief and I'll add it next month.

What if you're not available when I need you?
Capacity is shown live in the nav and on each tier card. If the next sprint slot is full I'll tell you up front, lock you in for the following window, and either suggest someone I'd hire for the gap or hold the brief warm. No pretending.

How is this not just "freelancer risk"?
Mutual NDA up front. Full IP assignment on delivery. Escrow available on builds over $20k. Code, design files, and docs in a repo you own from day one — so if I get hit by a bus, your team can keep shipping.

Can you work with my existing team?
Yes — most engagements are alongside an in-house team. I plug in as the senior generalist who owns end-to-end outcomes, while your engineers stay focused on the platform. I'll PR-review and document anything I ship.

What's the smallest engagement you take?
The 4-week sprint at $8k is the floor. Below that, it's me writing a paid strategy memo or doing a single advisory call. I don't take projects I can't ship inside a clean Friday-demo cadence.

How do you compare to an agency on day one?
You hear back from me, personally, inside one business day. There's no agency sales call. You get a written response to your brief, a calendar link, and — if it's a fit — a scoped proposal with weekly milestones inside 48 hours.

What does the first week actually look like?
Two long calls. A written brief in your words, sharpened. A clickable prototype by day 7. By Friday you have a sense of voice, architecture, and tempo — and you can call it off if the cadence isn't yours.

Where are you based and what timezones do you cover?
Karachi · UTC+5. Four hours of overlap with EU mornings and US east-coast afternoons. Most clients sync once a week and async the rest — and I reply inside one business day, every day.

What does the contract look like?
A two-page MSA + a one-page SOW per engagement. Mutual NDA, milestone billing, full IP on delivery. I'll send a sample after the intro call so you can run it past your counsel without any drama.
