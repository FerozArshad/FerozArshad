## Selected work

### FactFlow AI
**The problem:** verifying claims automatically means pulling from dozens of official data sources, and being able to trust the answer.

**What I built:** an LLM planner that routes each claim to 70+ government and financial APIs, with verifiability gates, a PostgreSQL ingestion pipeline and deterministic output settings. Live at [factflow.ai](https://factflow.ai).

`Python` `LLM` `PostgreSQL` `API integration` `Vercel`

### Dental Scotland: AI booking assistant
**The problem:** enquiries for a 6-location dental group arrive on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram, and nobody can answer them all instantly.

**What I built:** "Denny", an assistant that answers questions and turns them into booking leads on all three channels, with human takeover. Alongside it: a custom admin dashboard, WhatsApp OTP and payment reminders, a membership signup flow, and a full SEO and performance audit.

`Node.js` `Meta Cloud API` `WhatsApp` `Automation` `SEO`

### Kamus Dewan: Malay dictionary platform
**The problem:** a large dictionary needed a team to edit entries safely, with every change reversible.

**What I built:** a PostgreSQL-backed dashboard portal with full version history, preflight validation and one-step rollback.

`Python` `PostgreSQL` `Railway`
